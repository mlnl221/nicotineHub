# Code of Conduct

Nicotine Hub follows the [Contributor Covenant v3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- **Do:** be respectful, welcoming, and patient; accept constructive criticism gracefully; focus on what is best for the community.
- **Don't:** harass, insult, demean, or publish others' private information. Sexualized language or imagery is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, at the maintainer's discretion. Report incidents via a [private security advisory](https://github.com/mlnl221/nicotineHub/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@mlnl221](https://github.com/mlnl221) directly. All reports are kept confidential.
